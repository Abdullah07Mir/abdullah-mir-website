About me

Mobile engineer with a data science foundation.

I'm a Senior Flutter developer with a BSc in Computer Science and 3.5+ years of industry experience designing, building and releasing cross-platform apps for Android and iOS. Across 35+ app projects, in product teams and for international freelance clients, I have owned the full lifecycle, from architecture and state management through to App Store and Google Play release.

I mentor junior developers through code reviews, lead teams on selected projects, and work closely with backend, design and QA colleagues. I am equally comfortable meeting clients directly to gather requirements and present progress.

I hold an MSc in Data Science from the University of Salford, focused on machine learning, statistical analysis and big data. My dissertation was an industry-partnered project with Fildata, where I also spent four months as a remote data science intern: analysing datasets of 300K+ records in Python, benchmarking five classification models on an imbalanced problem, and building Power BI dashboards for non-technical stakeholders.

I am open to senior Flutter developer roles, data scientist roles and freelance Flutter projects, and to projects where a company or client wants machine learning built into a mobile app.
